Answer: PIR sensors rely on differential pyroelectric heat variation (ΔT) across segmented fresnel lenses. When a person sits motionless (e.g. reading or sleeping), the thermal differential decays to zero, causing the sensor to falsely report an empty room. Furthermore, PIR yields zero distance or coordinate information.

In contrast, FMCW (Frequency-Modulated Continuous-Wave) radar actively transmits electromagnetic chirps. Even a motionless occupant reflects the wave back with a measurable time delay (τ = 2R/c), producing a constant beat frequency (fb) that directly reveals distance. Furthermore, the 60 GHz sensor's phase analysis (Δφ) tracks sub-millimeter chest wall movements from involuntary breathing, guaranteeing permanent retention.
Answer: The ESP32 integrates an internal multiplexing crossbar called the GPIO Matrix. This decoupling layer allows internal hardware peripherals (such as UART0, UART1, and UART2) to be mapped to arbitrary physical package pins via silicon routing registers (e.g. Serial2.begin(115200, SERIAL_8N1, 25, 26)). This ensures true hardware-buffered UART with DMA and hardware FIFO ring-buffers, avoiding the CPU-blocking polling penalty of SoftwareSerial.

Why GPIO 9/10 are forbidden: On typical ESP32 modules (WROOM-32 / WROVER), GPIO 6 through 11 are connected internally to the high-speed SPI flash memory chip storing the bootloader and firmware code. Accessing GPIO 9 (SD2) or GPIO 10 (SD3) corrupts the SPI flash instruction stream, instantly plunging the microcontroller into an unrecoverable crash and watchdog reboot loop.

Answer: 24 GHz waves (λ ≈ 12.5 mm) penetrate surfaces easily but frequently produce multipath ghost reflections from metallic appliances, HVAC air drafts, and fluttering curtains. In contrast, 60 GHz waves (λ ≈ 5.0 mm) experience much higher atmospheric attenuation and tighter spatial reflection geometry.

By applying Euclidean spatial gating ($d = \sqrt{(X_{24} - X_{60})^2 + (Y_{24} - Y_{60})^2} \le 0.50\text{ m}$), a reflection visible only to 24G has no corresponding 60G point cluster within 0.50 meters. The fusion state machine recognizes this lack of spatial convergence, classifies the unassociated 24G return as a spurious reflection, and suppresses it—achieving zero false positives.
