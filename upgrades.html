RF WAVE PHYSICS & HOMODYNE MIXING

1. FMCW Chirp & Beat Frequency Physics

Frequency-Modulated Continuous-Wave radars emit linear frequency chirps. The time delay τ between the transmitted chirp and the received echo produces a constant intermediate beat frequency (fb) proportional to distance.

τ DELAY: 24.0 ns BEAT fb: 116.6 kHz ΔR RES: 3.75 cm

1. LINEAR CHIRP RAMP EQUATION
f(t) = f0 + S · t,   S = B / Tc

Synthesizer ramps frequency across 4 GHz sweep bandwidth B within chirp period Tc = 50 μs, producing slope S = 80 MHz/μs.

2. HOMODYNE MIXER DE-CHIRPING
fb = S · (2R / c) + (2vr / λ)

Multiplying the received echo with the local oscillator yields the constant intermediate beat frequency fb, directly proportional to range R.

3. RANGE-FFT RESOLUTION LIMIT
ΔR = c / (2B) = 3.75 cm

Applying a 512-point FFT on the beat signal extracts sharp range bin peaks. At B=4 GHz, targets separated by as little as 3.75 cm are resolved.

3D MULTI-TARGET ESTIMATION

2. 9-State Extended Kalman Filter & Hungarian Matching

Visualizing 3D spatial covariance ellipsoids contracting over consecutive updates with global Hungarian bipartite track assignment.

9-STATE KINEMATIC VECTOR
x = [x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, ax, ay, az]T

Incorporates 3D position, velocity, and acceleration derivatives to accurately track non-linear trajectories during human athletic maneuvers.

HUNGARIAN DATA ASSOCIATION
Cij = √((xi - zj)T S-1 (xi - zj))

Solves global assignment cost matrix via Kuhn-Munkres algorithm in O(N³), guaranteeing zero track-swapping when two individuals cross paths.
